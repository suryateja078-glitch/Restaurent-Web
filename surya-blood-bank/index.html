Why it matters

Blood can't be manufactured. It can only be given.

Every two seconds someone in India needs blood — from accident trauma to childbirth complications to cancer treatment. Hospitals rely on a steady donor pipeline, not just emergency appeals.

One donation, three patients

Whole blood is separated into red cells, plasma and platelets — a single unit can treat up to three people with different needs.

Shelf life is short

Platelets last 5 days and red cells around 42 — banks need continuous donors, not just a rush during a crisis.

18–65, healthy & willing

Most healthy adults meeting basic weight and haemoglobin criteria can donate every 90 days without any health risk.

How Surya Blood Bank works

From a search to a saved life, in four steps

1

Search or request

Patients or hospital staff search live stock by blood group and location, or raise an urgent request.

2

Match with donors

The system alerts nearby verified donors of a matching group and recent donation eligibility.

3

Screen & donate

Donors are screened at the partner hospital or camp — vitals, haemoglobin and a short health check.

4

Deliver & track

Units are logged into inventory and dispatched to the requesting hospital, tracked end to end.
