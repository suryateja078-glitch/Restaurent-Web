What "Healthy", "Limited" and "Critical" mean

  • Healthy

    More than 4 days of expected demand covered.

  • Limited

    1–4 days of cover — donors of this group are encouraged to come in soon.

  • Critical

    Less than a day of cover — active alerts are going out to matching donors.
