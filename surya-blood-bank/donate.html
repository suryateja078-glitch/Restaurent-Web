Before you register

Eligibility at a glance

This is a general guide — the final check happens at screening, done by a qualified nurse or doctor at the donation site.

  • Aged 18–65 and generally in good health
  • Weigh at least 50 kg
  • Haemoglobin of at least 12.5 g/dL on the day
  • Wait 90 days between whole-blood donations
  • Avoid donating with active fever, infection or during pregnancy
What to expect

The donation process, start to finish

1

Register

Fill the form below or sign in at the camp desk with a valid ID.

2

Screen

Quick check of weight, blood pressure, haemoglobin and health history.

3

Donate

The actual draw takes about 8–10 minutes, seated and supervised.

4

Rest & refresh

Relax for 15 minutes with a snack and fluids before you leave.
